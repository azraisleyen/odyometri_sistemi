# Hughson-Westlake Algorithm

Defaults: 250-8000 Hz, clinical order 1000, 2000, 4000, 8000, 1000 retest, 500, 250 Hz; start intensity 40 dB HL; intensity range -10 to 120 dB HL. Heard responses decrease intensity by 10 dB. No response or timeout increases intensity by 5 dB.

Threshold criteria are configurable: two out of three ascending responses or three out of five ascending responses. Only valid ascending trials are counted. Biomedical parameters are centralized in configuration records and `config/audiometry-config.json`.
